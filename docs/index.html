Words your Mac won't suggest

Misspell suicide, abortion or condom on a Mac and the spell checker has nothing for you: suicde, aborion, condm. It accepts all three words when they are spelled right. It just never offers them. Click any underlined word to see what your Mac offers instead.

How sure is this?

A spell checker misses the right word sometimes for ordinary reasons. To measure how often, the probe ran the full eight-typo battery on a random sample of words and counted how many typos brought each one back.

Run it on your Mac

The census is a Swift command-line tool that talks to the same spell checker TextEdit, Mail, Notes and Safari use. It needs Xcode's command-line tools and nothing else.

git clone https://github.com/TheWingTheWong/nosuggest
cd nosuggest
swift run -c release nosuggest word suicide lesbian
./scripts/run-census.sh   # about 45 minutes
